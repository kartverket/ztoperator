File name for ADRs should always be in the format `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`, where `YYYY-MM-DD` is the date of the decision
and `title` is a short, descriptive title for the decision. The title should be in lowercase and use hyphens to separate
words.

ADR template, used for more detailed decisions:

# [short title of solved problem and solution]

Status: [proposed | rejected | accepted | deprecated | … | superseded by [...](...)]  
Deciders: [list everyone involved in the decision]

## Context and Problem Statement
[Describe the context and problem statement, e.g., in free form using two to three sentences. You may want to articulate the problem in form of a question.]
